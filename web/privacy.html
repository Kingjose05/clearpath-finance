ClearPath Privacy

Last updated September 10, 2026.

Data handling

ClearPath stores financial accounts, transactions, settings, and Gmail access information locally in your browser or device. The hosted app does not operate an application database and does not sell or share financial data.

Gmail access

When you choose to connect Gmail, ClearPath requests read-only Gmail access. It reads matching bank-notification messages to identify transaction amounts, merchants, dates, transaction types, and the last four digits used to associate an account. ClearPath does not send email, delete email, or change your mailbox.

Your control

You can disconnect Gmail or clear all locally stored app data from Settings. You can also revoke access from your Google Account security settings.

Security

Google authorization occurs directly with Google. Never enter your Google password inside ClearPath. Because browser storage belongs to the browser profile, use a trusted device and enable its screen lock.
